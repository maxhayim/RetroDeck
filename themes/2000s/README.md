# The 2000s

## [Multi Control 2000](multi-control-2000/)

[![Multi Control 2000](multi-control-2000/tv-night.jpg)](multi-control-2000/)

A double-DIN unit from around 2000 that is nearly all display: a big blue fluorescent window whose bars fan round a half circle, keys down both sides and a chrome volume lever.

## [Twin Dial 2001](twin-dial-2001/)

[![Twin Dial 2001](twin-dial-2001/tv-night.jpg)](twin-dial-2001/)

A double-DIN unit from around 2001 with two round green dials in a wide silver frame, each ringed with fine spokes that follow the music: the volume on the left, the track on the right.

## [Rainbow 2002](rainbow-2002/)

[![Rainbow 2002, rendered](rainbow-2002/render-night.jpg)](rainbow-2002/)

A silver double-DIN unit from around 2002 with an in-dash CD changer, its wide window's bars running through the rainbow from the bass to the treble.

## [Blue Matrix 2004](blue-matrix-2004/)

[![Blue Matrix 2004, rendered](blue-matrix-2004/render-night.jpg)](blue-matrix-2004/)

A dark chrome double-DIN unit from around 2004 that is nearly all display: a big blue dot-matrix window, a strip of slim keys and two big polished knobs.
