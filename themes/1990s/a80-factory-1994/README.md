# A80 Factory 1994

**Era:** 1990s  ·  **Display:** Reflective LCD: dark segments on a panel lit green from behind, whatever the dash lighting  ·  **Skin id:** `a80factory94`

A car maker's factory CD and tape unit from the mid-1990s, in charcoal plastic: a CD slot across the top, a TUNE SEEK rocker beside six preset keys with the CD functions printed above them in orange and below in green, a cassette door, CD, AM·FM and TAPE keys down the side, and a sound panel with BASS, MID and TREB knobs beside the display. Modelled in 3D and rendered with Blender (see tools/blender/a80.py), on the factory unit fitted to the A80. At night the legends light green, like the display, whatever colour the dash is set to.

## Keys

**Working:**

- PUSH PWR·VOL: power
- The VOL knob
- TUNE SEEK ∨ and ∧: previous and next track
- Presets 1–6
- CD opens your music (SRC)
- TAPE plays and pauses
- SCAN cycles the visualizer (DISP)
- EQ opens the menu

**For the look only** (they do nothing):

- Both EJECT keys, AM·FM, LOUD
- The BASS, MID and TREB knobs

## Branding zones

Header (on the cassette door), corner (top right, beside the CD slot), faceplate (the brand line above the display), LCD, visualizer. A logo stays inside its zone and never covers a key.
