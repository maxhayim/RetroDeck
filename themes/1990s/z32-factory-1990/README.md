# Z32 Factory 1990

**Era:** 1990s  ·  **Display:** Vacuum fluorescent behind black glass, lit the same by day and night, in the illumination colour (the real unit's was cyan)  ·  **Skin id:** `z32factory90`

A car maker's factory stack from around 1990: a tuner and tape deck over a three-disc changer, in near-black satin plastic set into a carbon-look dash. Modelled on the factory tape deck and changer fitted to the Z32. The legends are white: printed paint by day, backlit at night.

## Keys

**Working:**

- EJECT opens your music (SRC)
- SCAN cycles the visualizer (DISP)
- PROG opens the menu
- PWR
- APS |<< and >>|
- The round PLAY/PAUSE key
- The tape keys, which double as presets: REW 1, STOP 2, FF 3, APS REW 4, NR 5, APS FF 6
- The VOL knob

**For the look only** (they do nothing):

- FM/AM, TUNE ▲ ▼, LOUD
- DISC SELECT 1 2 3, RPT, << and >>
- The BASS/BAL and TREB/FADER knobs

## Branding zones

Header (on the tape door), corner (top right), faceplate (top left of the changer), LCD, visualizer. A logo stays inside its zone and never covers a key.
